# Password and Remote Access Standard (draft v0.3)

Owner: IT Operations · Applies to: all staff and contractors

1. **Length.** Passwords must be at least 6 characters.
2. **Complexity.** No complexity rules; users may choose any password.
3. **Expiry.** Passwords never expire and may be reused.
4. **Lockout.** There is no account lockout, to avoid help-desk calls.
5. **Remote access.** Staff connect to the office VPN with their domain password. MFA is optional.
6. **Shared accounts.** Teams may share one account per department for convenience.
7. **Storage.** Service-account passwords are kept in `passwords.xlsx` on the shared drive.
